Who We Are

A Supply Chain Built From The Ground Up

Agri Out is building a scalable agricultural procurement infrastructure focused on direct farm sourcing, transparent quality standards, and reliable institutional supply — starting from the village level.

We operate through a field-based model: our team works directly with partner farmers, runs branch-level quality checks, and delivers verified produce to institutional buyers across hotels, catering services, and large-scale events.

Our Process

From Field To Institution — In Five Steps

01

Field Procurement

Our operations team visits partner farmers, checks crop availability, and procures directly at the farm level.

02

Branch Quality Check

Produce is transported to the nearest Agri Out branch for standardised quality verification.

03

Immediate Settlement

Farmers are paid immediately after quality check. Transport costs are fully covered by Agri Out.

04

Supply Coordination

Verified produce is sorted, packed, and allocated to active institutional orders.

05

Institutional Delivery

Fresh, verified produce reaches hotels, caterers, event kitchens, and food businesses on schedule.

Agri Out's agricultural support program is available to long-term supply partners.

After establishing a consistent supply relationship, partner farmers become eligible for structured support — including access to quality seeds, equipment, and working capital assistance.
